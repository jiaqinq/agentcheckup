# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume competence. Attack the idea, not the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space where you are representing this project.

## Expected

- Critique the code, the rule, or the reasoning — with specifics.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not. Most rule disagreements
  come from different deployment contexts, not from carelessness.
- Accept "no" on a PR gracefully. A declined rule is not a judgement of you.
- Give credit for work you build on.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, dismissiveness, or "this is obviously wrong" without explanation.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this, and may
block repeat offenders. If you need to report something, open a private security
advisory or contact the maintainer directly.

Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-specific code of conduct rather than a full adoption of
the Contributor Covenant. It is deliberately brief because a long document nobody
reads protects nobody.
